Privacy & Media Information

A clear explanation of the website's purpose and its use of community media.

Website purpose

This website shares educational information and media related to BIO COMMUNITY. It is a static informational website and does not provide user accounts, registration, payments, or interactive data-submission forms.

Personal information

The website has no built-in process for collecting student personal information. Standard hosting infrastructure may process technical request data as part of delivering the website.

Photos and videos

Community photographs and videos should be published only after the appropriate school and parent/guardian permissions have been obtained where required. The website should not be used to publish private student details.

External services

The site is designed without analytics, advertising trackers, embedded social feeds, or non-essential cookies. If external services are added later, this information must be reviewed and updated.

This page is informational and is not a substitute for an official school privacy policy. Confirm the wording and media permissions with the school before public launch.
